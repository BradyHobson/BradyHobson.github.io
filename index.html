About Me

My name is Brady Hobson, I grew up in Dudley, MA and I am currently looking for work.

I recently graduated from Northeastern University with a B.S. in Data Science and a minor in Mathematics. Here I spent my time studying to achieve a 3.85 GPA to graduate magna cum laude and be on the Dean's List every semester. I took many classes to enhance my skills in data analytics, data visualizations, data engineering, machine learning, and statistics. I also participated in clubs such as Survivor Northeastern and Husky Hunt.

During my time at Northeastern I committed multiple semesters to our Co-op program. My first Co-op was at Solid Biosciences working on data management and analytics to help the downstream processing team with their research of Duchenne Muscular Dystrophy. My second Co-op was at Wayfair working as a Software Engineer and headlined the development of a GenAI chatbot for the Supply Chain Team. At both of these working experiences I established leadership abilities and hands on experience of working with complex datasets to build scalable tools and provide insights to support the teams.
